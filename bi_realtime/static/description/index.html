AKREM BI Realtime

Turn your Odoo data into live dashboards — without leaving Odoo

AKREM BI Realtime is a native Business Intelligence application for Odoo 17. Monitor sales, finance, CRM, inventory, purchases, HR and Point of Sale from one interactive cockpit. When a quotation is confirmed, an invoice is posted or a lead is won, your KPIs and charts update automatically in real time — no export, no spreadsheet, no waiting.

Your command center

  • KPI cards with variation vs previous period

Real-time sync — your competitive edge

Unlike static reports, AKREM BI Realtime listens to your business events through Odoo Bus. Create or validate a sales order, customer invoice, stock move, purchase order or CRM opportunity: affected KPIs refresh without reloading the page.

LIVE badge on the dashboard — your team always works with fresh numbers.

Complete feature set

  • Custom KPIs — Odoo models, domains, aggregations (sum, count, average…)
  • DAX-style measures — advanced calculated indicators
  • KPI history — track performance over time
  • Period comparison — current vs previous period (N vs N-1)
  • Alert rules — automatic email when thresholds are reached
  • PDF reports — executive, sales, invoice, CRM, stock templates
  • AI assistant (optional) — ask questions about your data, configurable API URL
  • Proactive AI insights — suggestions surfaced in the app
  • Import / export — share dashboard configurations across databases
  • Scheduled refresh — cron jobs for batch KPI updates

Get started in 3 steps

  1. Install AKREM BI Realtime from the Apps menu (Odoo 17).
  2. Open Dashboard — use the setup wizard or default dashboards per department.
  3. Configure KPIs with source Odoo and the right model (e.g. sale.order, account.move).

Optional: set system parameter bi_realtime.ai_api_url to connect Groq or your AI API.
